# Theme Selection

- Basic: general training, seminars, balanced decks.
- Minimal: academic, quiet, content-focused decks.
- Colorful: events, youth audiences, creative topics.
- Dark: modern, evening, technology-adjacent decks.
- Gradient: visual, creative, high-impact decks.
- Tech: programming, developer, code-heavy decks.
- Business: proposals, reports, corporate decks.

If the user says "make it look good", infer from content:

- Business content: business
- Technical content: tech or dark
- Creative content: colorful or gradient
- General content: basic
